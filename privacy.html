Privacy Policy

A clear, plain-language explanation of how communications and inquiries are handled.

Last updated: October 2026

1. No Direct Data Collection on This Website

This website is a completely static online portfolio and informational site. It contains no web forms, no user tracking databases, no advertising pixels, and no analytics cookies. The site does not collect, record, or harvest personal data from visitors automatically.

2. Information You Choose to Share

If you decide to contact me via direct email (amalajose.work@gmail.com) or message me on LinkedIn, you voluntarily choose to share details such as your name, email address, company name, and details regarding your Microsoft IT environment.

Any information you share through these channels is used solely for:

  • Responding directly to your technical enquiry or consultation request.
  • Discussing prospective Microsoft Managed Service Provider (MSP) scopes.
  • Preparing a tailored support plan or proposal for your business.

3. We Never Sell Your Data

Your information is strictly confidential and is never sold, leased, rented, or shared with third parties or data brokers for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Deletion on Request

I retain correspondence only as long as needed to conduct business communications or service our ongoing client relationship. If you choose not to proceed with an MSP engagement, your messages are not retained indefinitely.

You may request that your contact details and communication history be updated or permanently deleted from my email records at any time. Simply send an email request to:

amalajose.work@gmail.com

All deletion requests are acknowledged and fulfilled promptly.

5. External Links

This website contains an external link to my professional LinkedIn profile. When you follow an external link, the privacy policies and practices of that respective platform apply.
