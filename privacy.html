Privacy Policy

A clear, plain-language explanation of how your privacy and information are respected.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction & Scope

This website is owned and operated by Amala Jose, an independent Microsoft Managed Service Provider (MSP) offering proactive remote Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft Intune, and Exchange Online management and support for organisations worldwide.

I believe that privacy should be straightforward, transparent, and built on trust. This document outlines how this website functions, why no personal data is collected when you browse, and how any information you voluntarily provide is handled.

2. Static Website with Zero Tracking

This is a 100% static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has been intentionally architected to eliminate unnecessary data collection, trackers, and telemetry:

  • No Cookies: This website sets no cookies whatsoever (no session cookies, tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party cookies).
  • No Analytics or Tracking Scripts: There are no Google Analytics, tracking pixels, tag managers, session replay tools, or behavioral profiling scripts loaded on this site.
  • No Forms or Web Trackers: This site contains no contact forms, automated lead capture scripts, or input fields that store your information in a database.
  • No Database or Backend Storage: There is no backend web server, database, or cloud repository capturing visitor logs or identifying records through this site.
  • No External Web Fonts or CDN Trackers: All styling and vector icons are embedded directly into the site's code, preventing third-party providers from logging your IP address or browser details when downloading fonts.

When you visit or navigate this site, you browse anonymously. No personal information is gathered, recorded, or processed by me through your visit.

3. Voluntary Communications

If you choose to contact me—such as by sending an email directly to amalajose.work@gmail.com or connecting with me through LinkedIn—you do so voluntarily.

Any contact information or details you provide in your message (such as your name, corporate email address, organisation name, time zone, or operational requirements) will be used solely for the following purposes:

  • To review your enquiry and respond directly to your technical questions.
  • To discuss potential Microsoft IT consulting, managed support partnerships, or IT assessment findings.
  • To communicate regarding scheduling, service scope, and technical proposals.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Your trust is paramount. I adhere to strict communication integrity:

  • Never Sold or Rented: Your contact information, correspondence, and technical details will never be sold, rented, monetised, or traded with data brokers or marketing lists.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your information is never shared with third-party vendors, affiliates, or marketers without your explicit prior written consent.
  • Confidentiality: Technical architecture details, tenant configurations, and organizational information shared during discussions or assessments are kept strictly confidential.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Email communications and correspondence are retained only as long as necessary to conduct ongoing business discussions, fulfill support contracts, or comply with standard record-keeping obligations.

You have full control over your data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any correspondence or information held about our communications.
  • Request correction or updating of any inaccurate contact details.
  • Request the immediate deletion of your correspondence and contact information from my active mailbox.

To exercise any of these rights, simply send an email request to amalajose.work@gmail.com. Requests will be addressed promptly.

6. External Links & Third-Party Services

This website includes external links to LinkedIn (to view my professional profile and connect). When you click an external link to LinkedIn or any other third-party platform, you leave this website and become subject to that platform's own terms of service and privacy policies. I encourage you to review LinkedIn's privacy policy before interacting with their platform.

7. Service Delivery Mode

All Microsoft Managed Service Provider (MSP) engagements, consultations, tenant administration, and IT support services are delivered 100% remotely for organisations worldwide across time zones. I do not provide on-site visits, in-person office repairs, or physical hardware services.
