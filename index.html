Managed Microsoft IT Services

Focused, security-driven remote management for your cloud identity, modern endpoints, collaboration tools, and email infrastructure.

Microsoft Entra ID

Comprehensive identity and access management ensuring that the right employees have secure, authenticated access to company resources.

  • Enterprise applications and app registrations configuration.
  • Identity Governance, entitlement management, and access reviews.
  • Privileged Identity Management (PIM) for least-privilege security.
  • Single Sign-On (SSO), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and Conditional Access.
Backed by SC-300 certification.

Microsoft Intune

Unified endpoint management to standardize, secure, deploy software, and continuously manage all Windows and macOS devices.

  • Windows and macOS device management and compliance policies.
  • Configuration profiles, security baselines, and restriction policies.
  • Application deployment and Windows Autopilot automated provisioning.
  • Operating system patch and software update management.

Microsoft 365 Admin

Ongoing tenant administration and configuration to maintain a secure, collaborative, and clean productivity environment.

  • Tenant administration, domain configuration, and license management.
  • User lifecycle management: structured onboarding and offboarding workflows.
  • Microsoft Secure Score analysis, hardening, and continuous improvement.
  • Teams, SharePoint Online, and OneDrive collaboration governance.

Exchange Online

Reliable enterprise email operations, mail flow protection, and anti-phishing defense to safeguard communication channels.

  • Mail flow rules, transport policies, and message routing management.
  • Anti-phishing, anti-spam, and malware defense policy configuration.
  • SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication configuration and monitoring.
  • Shared mailboxes, distribution lists, and mailbox permission governance.

Support Schedule & Scope

When I'm Available

  • Agreed regular business hours tailored to your organisation's time zone.

  • Agreed upon upfront and written into your support agreement.
  • Direct, accountable contact without call-centre queues.

What's Included

  • Remote administration and tenant configuration.
  • Proactive management and continuous monitoring.
  • Scheduled configuration updates and patch rollouts.
  • Identity and security reviews across Entra ID & Intune.
