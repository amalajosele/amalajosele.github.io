Managed Microsoft IT Services

Focused, security-driven remote management for your cloud identity, modern endpoints, collaboration tools, and email infrastructure.

Microsoft Entra ID

Comprehensive identity and access management ensuring that the right employees have secure, authenticated access to company resources.

  • Enterprise applications and app registrations configuration.
  • Identity Governance, entitlement management, and access reviews.
  • Privileged Identity Management (PIM) for least-privilege security.
  • Single Sign-On (SSO), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), and Conditional Access.
Backed by SC-300 certification.

Microsoft Intune

Unified endpoint management to standardize, secure, deploy software, and continuously monitor all Windows and macOS devices.

  • Windows and macOS device enrollment and compliance policies.
  • Application deployment and Windows Autopilot provisioning.
  • BYOD security policies and mobile application protection.
  • Automated operating system patch and update management.

Microsoft 365 (Office 365)

Ongoing tenant administration and configuration to maintain a secure, collaborative, and clean productivity environment.

  • Tenant setup, domain configuration, and license administration.
  • Microsoft Teams collaboration governance and security settings.
  • SharePoint Online architecture and OneDrive permissions management.
  • Structured, repeatable user onboarding and offboarding workflows.

Exchange Online

Reliable enterprise email operations, mail flow protection, and anti-phishing defense to safeguard communication channels.

  • Mail flow routing, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC record management.
  • Spam, malware, and anti-phishing protection policies.
  • Shared mailboxes, aliases, and distribution list governance.
  • Email delivery issue investigation and hygiene maintenance.
100% Remote Delivery: All MSP services, ongoing management, user lifecycle workflows, and tenant administration are conducted entirely remotely. I do not provide on-site visits, physical hardware repairs, or local network cable installations.
